Retention · Reactivation · July 2026 – ongoing

Platinum Member Reactivation

Instead of only sending a discount, I set the team one question: of the members who stopped booking, how many still traveled but chose another platform because of route or price? We paired the email, web and app offer with session and route data, then had the reservations team call hundreds of members directly.

837lapsed members on the call list, across four behavior segments
Ongoingreservations team calling lapsed members every week

The headline finding: price was rarely the reason. Most lapsed members simply had no trip planned, or booked elsewhere out of habit or convenience. So the fixes focused on making membership visible and checkout easier rather than on deeper discounts, plus regular personal contact with lapsed members.

Sponsorship · Partnerships · 2026 – 2027

Trail running sponsorships

I turned event sponsorship into a sales channel. Flymya became Silver Sponsor of three trail races and sold the trip around the run, not just a logo on a banner: flights, hotels, transfers and packages, each with an exclusive runner offer.

Aug – Oct 2026Pyin Oo Lwin Jungle TrailRunner deals on flights, hotels and bus + hotel packages

13 – 14 Feb 2027Kalaw Trail RunCo-branded booking page for international runners

At the Taunggyi race village we gave merchandise for bookings (a tote bag for flights, a mug for hotels) and a welcome voucher for every new sign-up. For Kalaw, we built a single landing page on the organizer’s registration site covering flights into Myanmar, tourist visas, e-SIMs, transfers, blocked hotel rooms and onward trips to Inle and Bagan.
